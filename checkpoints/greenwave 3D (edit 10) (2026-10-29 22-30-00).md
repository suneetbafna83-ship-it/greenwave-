## 🌊 Key Architectural Deliverables

### 1. Refined Meandering River with Perpendicular Channel Width
- **Normal Vector Channel Expansion**: Replaced linear X-offsets with true curve-normal trigonometry (`riverBankX(z)`), guaranteeing a uniform 38-unit wide channel across all curves without pinching or warping.
- **Sunken Riverbed & Deep Channel**: Water surface sits sunken at `y = -0.55` over a dark aquatic silt riverbed floor at `y = -2.4`.
- **Granite Quay Embankment Walls**: Continuous vertical stone retaining walls lining both the West Bank and East Bank from street level (`y = 0.08`) down to the riverbed.
- **Riverwalk Promenade**: Tops of both quays feature stone balustrades with illuminated glowing promenade lighting.
- **Dynamic Water Shimmer**: Animated surface specular flow in `useFrame` reacting to solar angles and nighttime city bloom.

### 2. Monumental Full-Span Bridges (Bank-to-Bank)
- **True River Span**: Expanded bridge lengths from the old 18-unit stub to **62 world units**, completely spanning the 38m water channel with heavy reinforced concrete anchor abutments on both shores.
- **Grand Suspension Bridge (`iz = 13` — Grand Suspension Way)**:
  - Twin soaring 32m Gothic-Cyber H-pylons with triple cross-bracing and navigation warning strobe lights.
  - 3D Catmull-Rom catenary main suspension cables sweeping from shore anchors over pylon saddles and dipping to midspan.
  - 28 vertical steel suspender hanger rods connecting main cables to the bridge deck.
  - Under-deck concrete riverbed foundation piers with maritime clearance.
  - Glowing emerald-cyan aerodynamic safety barrier conduits.
- **North Tied-Arch Bridge (`iz = 9` — North Bridge Crossing)**:
  - Sweeping parabolic structural steel through-arches (15m rise) with vertical wire hangers and illuminated cyan ribbing.
- **South Cable-Stayed Bridge (`iz = 18` — South Bridge Crossing)**:
  - Futuristic 26m inverted-Y diamond needle pylon with 28 radiant fan stay cables and neon magenta lighting.
- **Expressway Viaducts**: Multi-span continuous box girders supported by heavy cylindrical concrete riverbed piers with pier cap beams.

### 3. Tactical 2D Radar Minimap Sync
- Integrated `riverBankX(z)` into `Minimap2D.jsx` to render the smooth river contours and added color-coded bridge markers.
