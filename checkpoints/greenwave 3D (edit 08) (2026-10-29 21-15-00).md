- **Architectural Suspension Bridges (`RiverBridges`)**:

### 3. Dramatic Directional Sunlight Shading on Buildings
- **Resolved UI Background Issue**: Fixed the problem where only the background color changed while building facades stayed flat and dark.
- **Directional Sunlight Dominance**:
  - Reduced overpowering ambient and hemisphere light, elevating the directional sun intensity to `2.85 – 3.2`.
  - Sun sweeps across a wide 380-unit orbital arc with realistic solar elevation and azimuth.
- **Physically Reactive Building Materials**:
  - **Golden Hour Dawn (06:00 – 08:30)**: East-facing skyscraper walls blush in warm golden sunlight (`#fde047`) with long dramatic shadows.
  - **Midday Direct Glare (12:00)**: Crisp overhead white sunlight illuminates rooftops and facades with specular highlights.
  - **Fiery Sunset (17:30 – 19:30)**: West-facing facades blaze in rich amber/crimson sunlight (`#f97316`), while East walls sink into twilight purple shadows.
  - **Window Emissive Modulation**: Window emissive intensity automatically drops to `0.12` during peak daylight (so building walls catch sunlight instead of acting like self-illuminating bulbs), and flares up to `1.2` at night for vivid cyberpunk neon.

### 4. Strict Slum vs. Extreme Urban Citadel Separation
- **Urban Zoning Rule**: Slums are **strictly barred** from being anywhere near extreme urban areas.
- **Extreme Urban Cyber Citadel**:
  - Concentrated in the North-West core (`x: -200 to -60`, `z: -200 to -60`).
  - Soaring glass skyscrapers up to 58m tall, pristine roads with 0 potholes.
- **Natural River & Commercial Buffer**: The meandering river and a wide commercial mid-rise zone create a natural 200m+ geographical barrier.
- **Far Peripheral Slums (Outer Badlands)**:
  - Confined strictly to the far South/South-East outer boundary (`distToCyber > 210` world units away).
  - Heavy crater clusters (3 to 6 potholes per segment) that penalize routing, demonstrating how GreenWave pre-flushes clean express corridors across the Grand Suspension Bridge instead.
