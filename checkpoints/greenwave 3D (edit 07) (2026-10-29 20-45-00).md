## 🌟 Major Architectural Additions

### 1. Organic Undefined Slum Distribution
- Replaced the rigid rectangular boundary (`ix >= 15 && iz >= 15`) with a continuous multi-frequency noise field `isSlumArea(ix, iz)`.
- Informal settlements now spread in organic, undulating pockets and corridors across the city without hard geometric edges.
- Roads weaving through these undefined pockets suffer severe crater clusters (3 to 6 potholes per segment), while main cyber avenues maintain a pristine 0-pothole standard.

### 2. Refined Bidirectional & True-Speed Road Traffic Indication
- Fixed directional oversight where road status only inspected single travel directions (`d=0` or `d=2`).
- Implemented `getRoadStatus(ix, iz, axis)` which:
  - Aggregates **both travel directions** across physical road segments (Eastbound + Westbound, Southbound + Northbound).
  - Inspects real-time vehicle speeds: counts stopped and crawling vehicles.
  - Combines physical queue length with pothole crater delays.
  - 🟢 **Green (Free)**: 0 to 1 moving vehicle with smooth open road.
  - 🟡 **Yellow (Busy)**: 2 to 3 vehicles or slowing queue.
  - 🔴 **Red (Gridlock / Jam)**: 2+ stopped/crawling vehicles, 4+ packed cars, or severe crater congestion.
- Both the 3D road overlay ribbons and the floating 2D tactical radar minimap now reflect live traffic state with 100% precision.

### 3. Continuous 24-Hour Timeline & Natural Diurnal Traffic System
- Completely removed the 3 discrete buttons (`Day`, `Sunset`, `Night`).
- Implemented a continuous mathematical 24-hour solar cycle (`compute24HourLighting(hour)`):
  - Continuous sun/moon orbital trajectory across the celestial dome.
  - Smooth trigonometric interpolation of solar elevation, sky color, fog, and ambient light.
  - Real-time window glass opacity and night-mode emissive window bloom.
- **Natural Diurnal Traffic Demand Curve (`getNaturalDemand(hour)`)**:
  - **Morning Rush Peak (07:30 – 09:30)**: Traffic surges up to ~650–700 vehicles.
  - **Noon Lull (12:00 – 14:00)**: Moderate, lower traffic (~280–340 vehicles).
  - **Evening Rush Peak (17:00 – 19:45)**: Severe gridlock demand (~680–740 vehicles).
  - **Night Quiet (23:00 – 05:00)**: Minimum traffic (~140–180 vehicles).
- **Interactive Scrubber & Presets**:
  - Interactive slider (`00:00` to `23:59`) in the top HUD to scrub to any time of day instantly.
  - Digital clock readout with AM/PM and commute phase tags.
  - Play/Pause button for automatic 24-hr clock progression.
  - Quick jump buttons for `08:30 Rush`, `13:00 Noon`, `18:15 Rush`, and `01:30 Night`.
- **Unpredictable Random Traffic Surges**:
  - An autonomous Poisson/Markov event generator triggers unpredictable flash gridlock (+220 vehicles injected) at unexpected intervals.
  - Added a manual `[⚡ Surge]` button to trigger unpredictable traffic jams on demand.

### 4. Zero-GC Smooth Camera Rig & Frame Drop Elimination
