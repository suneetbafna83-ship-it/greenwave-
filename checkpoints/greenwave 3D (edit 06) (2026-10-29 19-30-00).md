### 3. Asymmetric Pothole Distribution & Route Judgement
- **Road Condition Reality**:
  - Cyber avenues have **zero potholes**.
  - Slum streets have **dense clusters of 3 to 6 craters per block** (severe damage).
- **Dynamic A* Corridor Router Impact**:
  - Slum potholes carry heavy edge impedance (`+2.4` penalty per crater).
  - When dispatching from Sector 9 (Slum Post) or Sector 7, the AI corridor router actively avoids the broken slum streets and reroutes through the clean cyber arterial expressways, dynamically proving why digital twins improve emergency response times.

### 4. Live Road Congestion Filter (Green / Yellow / Red)
- Real-time road segment classification via `sim.getSegmentStatus(ix, iz, d)`:
  - 🟢 **Green**: Free-flowing traffic (queue length `< 2`, no slum craters).
  - 🟡 **Yellow**: Busy road (queue length `= 2` or moderate hazard presence).
  - 🔴 **Red**: Critical gridlock or heavy slum pothole cluster (queue length `≥ 3` or heavy crater blockage).
- **3D City Heatmap Overlay**:
  - 1,200 segment ribbons rendered with Three.js `instancedMesh` directly on the road surface.
  - Updates smoothly at 60 FPS.
  - 1-click toggle button `[🚦 Traffic Filter (Red/Yellow/Green)]` in both the top floating HUD bar and the left control switches.
